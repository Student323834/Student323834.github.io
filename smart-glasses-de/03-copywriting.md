1. **Problem**: German enterprises want hands-free AI efficiency, but bulky headsets cause fatigue and camera-heavy consumer glasses trigger strict GDPR and *Betriebsrat* roadblocks.
2. **Problem**: Technicians and mobile professionals lose critical minutes switching between tools, manuals, and handheld screens.
3. **Solution**: KlarSicht combines an ultra-lightweight (<42g) everyday frame with a sharp waveguide HUD and privacy-first AI assistant.
4. **Differentiation**: Unlike social-media camera glasses or heavy industrial visors, KlarSicht is camera-free by default, EU-hosted, and prescription-ready.
5. **Benefit**: Faster field-service execution, lower operational error rates, and smooth legal/works-council sign-off across Germany.
