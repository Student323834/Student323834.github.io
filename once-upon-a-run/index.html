How to Play

🖐

Change lane

Move your hand left, middle or right. Your hand position = your lane.

🖐

Jump

Swipe your hand quickly upwards, then bring it back calmly.

🖐

Slide

Swipe your hand quickly downwards, then bring it back calmly.

✌️⚡

Sprint

Hold a peace sign. Faster, double distance points – but costs stamina.

🖐🖐🛡️

Magic shield

Show both hands for a moment. Blocks the next hit for 5 s (15 s cooldown).

✋

Pause

Take your hand out of view – the game pauses. Show it again to continue.

💥 Hitting an obstacle makes you stumble and the Royal Guard catches up.
🏃 Running cleanly lets the guard fall back again.
⚠️ Two hits within 5 seconds – or the guard reaching you – means game over.
🪙 Coins 10 · 💎 Crystals 25 · ⭐ Stars 50 points + 1 point per metre.
